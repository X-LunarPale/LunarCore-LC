The official release's fabric.mod.json and its Modrinth project metadata
identify this release as MIT-licensed. Older repository branches identify
older releases as CC0; they are not used as the license for this artifact.

tectonic-model.json contains the mod's density functions, noise parameters,
noise settings and five mountain surface rules. tectonic-features.json
contains the upstream underground-river features, placed features, twelve
ore/gravel placement overrides, carver overrides, modifier specifications
and ultrasmooth density overlay. They are reproducibly extracted from the
pinned artifact with extract-model.ps1 and extract-features.ps1.
The presence of an original modifier specification does not imply that
every operation in that specification is implemented by this port.

Vanilla 26.3 density, material and feature dependencies are read from the
running Paper server's own resources or registries. Minecraft resources,
mod classes, a loader, datapack metadata, a data pack ZIP and a Lithostitched
runtime are not included or installed.

LunarCore adapts Tectonic's ConfigConstant, ConfigNoise and ConfigClamp
operations into native 26.3 density functions. Thirty-four advanced
options, base height and scale options, and the five official parameter
presets are resolved independently for each new world. ConfigNoise retains
the pinned artifact's float point arithmetic and normal/alternate volume
sampling order. Its interval bounds conservatively include configured
multipliers and offsets. Tectonic parameter noises retain their own
parameters and holder keys, while native samplers apply the mod's seed-name
aliases, including parameter/ridge -> minecraft:ridge.

LunarCore also adapts the upstream island-disable predicate, ultrasmooth
overlay, underground-river lichen, lanterns/chains and ice placement, ore
height-stabilized counts, and suppression of the three vanilla carvers
covered by the official no-carvers overlay. Decorations and generation
settings are attached to an individual generator without replacing shared
biomes.

The fixed release's spaghetti_enabled configuration key is not handled by
ConfigState.getValue and therefore resolves to zero. Its unused
alternate_continents_scaling flag is not exposed; the effective
alternate_erosion_scaling flag affects both continent and erosion noise as
it does in the artifact.

The native startup agent adapts per-world temperature queries, client
snowlines, ocean monument offsets during generation and reload, and
old-chunk blending and height compatibility. The module also implements
the frozen-ocean climate replacement, configurable minimum/maximum world
bounds, the minY-relative lava floor, /tectonic debug and the eight
terrain locators. WebUI supplies configuration in place of the mod's
client screens. The port does not promise block-for-block identity with
the original mod.
